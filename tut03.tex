      signed error, using computed minus target
    \item Verify that the central first-derivative approximation is

  \end{enumerate}

\begin{eg}[Second-Order Euler by Hand]{second-order-euler-by-hand}
  Consider the initial-value problem
  \[
    \forall x\in\RR,\quad \DiffOp^2(f)(x)=-f(x),\qquad
    f(0)=1,\qquad \DiffOp(f)(0)=0
  \]
  Apply forward Euler with $x_0=0$, step $h=\frac12$, and $N=2$ steps
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Introduce $v\defeq\DiffOp(f)$ and rewrite the equation as a
      system of two first-order ODEs. Identify $g(x,y,v)$ in the
      \xref[second-order Euler convention]{lec03::conv:second-order-euler}
    \item State the types and initial values of $f^\dagger$ and $f'$.
      Write their Euler updates. Which values must be used on the
      right-hand sides at each step?
    \item Calculate both steps and complete the table
      \[
        \begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
          n & x_n & f^\dagger(x_n) & f'(x_n)\\
          \hline
          0 & 0       & \phantom{00000} & \phantom{00000}\\
          1 & \frac12 &                 &                 \\
          2 & 1       &                 &
        \end{array}
      \]
    \item For comparison, the exact solution is $f(x)=\cos(x)$.
      Using radians, calculate the signed errors in $f^\dagger(1)$
      and $f'(1)$. Use the correct exact target for each
    \item Match your two tracked functions and initial values to
      $U(0)$, $U(1)$, and $a$ in the
      \xref[general-order convention]{lec03::conv:general-order-euler}
  \end{enumerate}
